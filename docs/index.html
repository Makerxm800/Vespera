Made for everyday browsing

Privacy tools that say what they do.

Vespera is designed to be useful without making absolute promises about online privacy.

Block known trackers and ads

Bundled filter lists stop matching requests before they load. See real blocked-request counts in the browser and make per-site exceptions.

Less tracking in the address bar

Vespera can remove common tracking parameters, prefer HTTPS, and send Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals. Sites decide whether to honor those signals.

Tabs that fit your workflow

Choose a horizontal strip or vertical sidebar, group related tabs, pin favorites, and bring back tabs from your last session.

A browser that feels like yours

Pick a theme and accent, tune a wallpaper, and adjust tab behavior, motion, and reading preferences in Settings.

Useful tools included

Reader View, Dark Pages, Focus Mode, downloads, and a privacy hub are built in — no extension store or account required.

Open source, community-led

Read the code, report a bug, or help shape the roadmap. Vespera is distributed under the MIT License.

Clear about the boundaries

Protection is not invisibility.

Here is what the current desktop build does — and what it does not claim to do.

What Vespera does

  • Blocks requests that match its bundled tracker and ad rules; no list catches every tracker.
  • Supports common tracking-link cleanup, HTTPS upgrades, and GPC/DNT signals.
  • Does not include usage analytics or telemetry in the app.
  • Keeps ordinary settings, bookmarks, and history on your device unless you choose account sync.

What Vespera does not promise

  • It does not currently spoof or randomize browser fingerprinting signals.
  • GPC and DNT are requests to websites, not enforcement.
  • The current desktop build does not run Chrome extensions.
  • Private windows reduce saved local activity; websites, networks, and internet providers may still identify you.
Optional sync: when you sign in, Vespera syncs settings, bookmarks, and history with the Vespera service. Sync is not end-to-end encrypted in this release. Do not sync information you do not want stored by that service.
System web engine: the desktop app uses the platform webview instead of bundling a single browser engine: Chromium-based WebView2 on Windows, WKWebView on macOS, and WebKitGTK on Linux. The system webview does not provide a Chrome extension runtime in this release.
